How to connect
  1. In the Twilio Console copy your Account SID and Auth Token (Dashboard > Account Info).
  2. Put them in .env as TWILIO_ACCOUNT_SID and TWILIO_AUTH_TOKEN, plus TWILIO_WHATSAPP_FROM (your WhatsApp number as whatsapp:+...). Redeploy.
  3. In Twilio: Messaging > Senders > WhatsApp senders > your number, set Incoming messages to the webhook above (HTTP POST). Delivery updates need no setup.
  4. Templates: create and get one approved in Twilio's Content Template Builder, then register its Content SID (HX...) under Templates with channel WhatsApp.
  5. Send the test below. New conversations must start with an approved template; once someone writes to you, free text is allowed for 24 hours.
