# Companies

The organisations your people work for.

## What it is for

A company groups its people, deals, notes and tasks in one place, and shows how much open business you have with it.

## How to use it

1. Open **Companies** to see every account with its number of people and open deal value.
2. Click **New company** to add one, or just type a company name when you add a person: the company is linked or created for you.
3. Open a company to see its **people**, **deals**, **notes** (including notes on its people) and **tasks**.
4. Use the company page to attach or detach people, add notes and tasks, or start a deal.

## Good to know

* A person belongs to at most one company.
* Companies are matched by **website domain** (or name), so entering the website helps avoid duplicates.
* **Deleting a company keeps its people.** They simply have no company afterwards.
* Companies can have tags ([Tags](/help/tags)) and custom fields ([Fields & pipelines](/help/fields)).
