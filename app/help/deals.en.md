# Deals

Track potential sales through your pipeline stages.

## The board

*Deals* shows a **board**: one column per stage with the number of deals and their total value.

* **Drag** a card to another column to move it. Dropping on *Lost* asks for a reason.
* *List* view sorts and filters by status, owner and close date.
* The header shows **open value**, **weighted value** (amount × the stage's win probability) and **won value**.

## Creating a deal

Click **New deal** on the Deals page, or on a person or a company. A deal has an amount and currency, an expected close date, an owner, a source, custom fields, a main contact and other **people with roles** (decision maker, champion...).

## Working on a deal

* On the deal page, the stage bar moves it forward.
* **Won** and **Lost** close it with a date.
* Notes and tasks attach to the deal.

## Good to know

* The stages and their probabilities are set under [Fields & pipelines](/help/fields). You can have several pipelines.
* Positive replies in the [Inbox](/help/inbox) are a good moment to create a deal.
