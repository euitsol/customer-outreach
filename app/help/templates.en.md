# Templates

Reusable message texts for email and WhatsApp.

## What it is for

Save an email you want to reuse across campaigns or inbox replies, so you do not retype it.

## How to use it

1. Click **New template**.
2. Give it a **Name** and choose the **Channel**: Email or WhatsApp.
3. For email, write a **Subject** and **Body**. You can use merge fields such as `{{first_name|there}}`, `{{company}}` or any imported column.
4. Click **Save template**. Use **Edit** or the bin icon on the list to change or delete one.

Email templates can be loaded into campaign stages ([Campaigns](/help/campaigns)) and inbox replies ([Inbox](/help/inbox)).

## WhatsApp templates

WhatsApp only lets you start a conversation (or write after 24 hours of silence) with a template that Meta has **approved**. Create it in your Meta Business account first, then register it here: enter the **WhatsApp template name** exactly as in Meta and its **Language** (for example `en_US`).

## Good to know

Changing a template does not change campaigns that already loaded it; the text was copied into the stage.
