# Tasks

Your follow-ups: what to do, for whom and by when.

## What it is for

Tasks make sure no lead is forgotten. A task can belong to a person, a company or a deal.

## How to use it

1. **Add a task** from this page, or from a person, company or deal page. Give it a title, a due date and time, a priority and an assignee.
2. Use the tabs to see **Due today**, **Overdue**, **All open** or **Done**.
3. Switch between **Mine** and **Everyone's** tasks.
4. Tick the box to complete a task.

## Good to know

* The dashboard shows your follow-ups, and the menu shows how many tasks are due.
* Log every call and meeting on the person's timeline with a one-line summary; see [People](/help/people).
* Tasks are saved with the record they belong to, so they show up on the person, company or deal page too.
