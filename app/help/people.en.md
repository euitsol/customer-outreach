# People

Everyone you talk to: leads, prospects and customers, with or without a campaign.

## Finding people

Use the search box (name, email, phone, job title, company) and the filters: lifecycle stage, lead status, owner, tag, campaign ("Not in any campaign" is handy), has email or phone, and "replied to a campaign". The search box at the top of the left menu searches people from any page.

## Adding a person

1. Click **Add person**.
2. Give at least a name, an email or a phone number.
3. If the email already exists, you are taken to the existing record. **People are never duplicated by email.**
4. Typing a company name links (or creates) the company. A company website sets its domain, which is how companies are matched later.

## The person page

* **Header**: name, lifecycle stage, company, email, phone, owner and where they came from.
* **Send a message**: email or WhatsApp, sent immediately from your connected account and saved in the [Inbox](/help/inbox). Emails continue the previous thread unless you type a subject.
* **Timeline**: everything that happened: notes, calls, meetings, emails and WhatsApp messages in and out, campaign emails (sent, opened, clicked, replied) and tasks. Use *Log a call, meeting or note* to add an entry.
* **Profile**: names, primary email and phone, job title, company, lifecycle stage, lead status, owner, source, tags, custom fields and the **Do not contact** switch.
* **Tasks, Deals, Campaigns**: what is open for this person. You can enroll them in a campaign from here.
* **Emails, phones & profiles**: a person can have several addresses and numbers. The ★ makes one primary. A bounced address is marked *bounced*.
* **Consent**: email and WhatsApp permission. "Opt out" of email stops all their campaigns.

**Lifecycle stage** describes the relationship: Subscriber → Lead → Marketing qualified → Sales qualified → Opportunity → Customer → Evangelist. **Lead status** is your working status while qualifying: New, Open, In progress, Contacted, Qualified, Unqualified, Bad timing.

## Bulk actions

Tick people (or *Select all N matching* after ticking the page) to enroll them in a campaign, add or remove tags, set the lifecycle stage, assign an owner, merge two, export, or delete.

## Duplicates and merging

On a person, **Merge** shows likely duplicates (same email domain, last name, phone or company). Merging moves every email address, campaign, note, task, conversation, deal and tag onto the record you keep, then deletes the other. If both were in the same campaign, the one that got further is kept.

> A delete or merge cannot be undone from the app. The [audit log](/help/audit) shows who did it.

## Good to know

* To add many people at once, use [Import](/help/import).
* Labels for grouping people are managed under [Tags](/help/tags).
