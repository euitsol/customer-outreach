# Do not contact

The global block list: addresses, domains and numbers that are never contacted.

## What it is for

Anything on this list is skipped by every campaign. It protects you from emailing people who asked not to be contacted, and keeps you compliant.

## How it fills up

* **Unsubscribes**: every campaign email has an unsubscribe link and the one-click `List-Unsubscribe` header. Unsubscribing adds the address here.
* **Hard bounces**: an address that does not exist is marked bounced and blocked.
* **You**, manually, using the form.

## How to use it

1. To block someone, type one email or domain per line under **Add entries**. A bare domain such as `competitor.com` blocks everyone at that domain. Add a reason and click **Add to list**.
2. Search the list by address or domain.
3. Click **Remove** to allow contact again.

## Good to know

* A person's own **Do not contact** switch (on their [People](/help/people) page) blocks every channel for them, including WhatsApp.
* Keep your daily limit and delays conservative on new sending domains; replies and engagement matter more than volume ([Sending settings](/help/settings)).
