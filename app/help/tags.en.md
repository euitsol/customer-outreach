# Tags

Labels shared by people and companies.

## What it is for

Tags let you group records any way you like: "Ireland", "hotel", "met at event".

## How to use it

* **Add tags** in bulk from the [People](/help/people) page, on a single record, or through the CSV [Import](/help/import).
* This page lists every tag with the number of people and companies that have it. Click a count to see them.
* **Rename** a tag by editing its name and clicking *Save*.
* **Delete** a tag with the bin icon. The people and companies keep existing; they just lose the label.

## Good to know

Renaming a tag to the name of another tag **merges** them.
