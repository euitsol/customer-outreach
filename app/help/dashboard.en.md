# Dashboard

Your daily starting point: what needs attention and how campaigns are doing.

## What it is for

The dashboard summarises the whole CRM on one page so you can decide what to do first.

## What you will see

* **Pipeline numbers**: open deal value, weighted value (amount × stage probability) and won value.
* **My follow-ups**: your tasks that are due or overdue, with a link to all tasks.
* **Unread messages**: inbox threads waiting for an answer.
* **Campaign email, last 14 days**: a chart of what was sent, opened and replied to.
* **Campaigns**: each campaign with its status and progress. If there are none, a button creates your first one.
* **Next up**: the next emails due from active campaigns.
* **System**: whether sending is switched on, SMTP is configured and the worker is alive.
* **Enrollments** and **Recent email activity**: the latest movement, with links to the full lists.

## How to use it

1. Check the **System** box first. If sending is off or the worker is not running, nothing goes out.
2. Work through **My follow-ups** and **Unread messages**.
3. Click any number or card to open the full list behind it.

## Good to know

* Nothing is sent while a campaign is a draft or paused. See [Campaigns](/help/campaigns).
* If emails are not going out, see the troubleshooting list in [Outbox](/help/outbox).
