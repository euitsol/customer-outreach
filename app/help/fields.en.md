# Fields & pipelines

Add your own fields to records and shape your sales stages.

## Custom fields

Extra fields for people, companies and deals.

1. Choose what the field belongs to (people, companies or deals).
2. Give it a **Label** and a **Type**: text, long text, number, date, dropdown, multiple choice, yes/no or link.
3. For dropdowns and multiple choice, enter the **Options**, one per line.
4. Save. The field appears on forms, in exports, in the API and as a merge field.

Examples: "Booking engine" (dropdown) on people, "Rooms" (number) on companies.

## Pipelines

A pipeline is the list of stages a deal moves through.

* The default is New lead → Contacted → Qualified → Proposal sent → Negotiation → Won / Lost.
* Edit the stage names, their **win probability** and their order.
* Add another pipeline if you sell different things.

Win probability drives the weighted forecast on [Deals](/help/deals) and the [Dashboard](/help/dashboard).

## Good to know

Only **owners and admins** can change fields and pipelines.
