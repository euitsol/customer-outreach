# Inbox

Every email reply and WhatsApp chat in one place, one thread per person and channel.

## What it is for

The inbox is where you answer people. Replies to your campaign emails and incoming WhatsApp messages arrive here, next to the emails you sent, so you always see the whole conversation.

## How to use it

1. Pick a thread on the left. Unread threads are highlighted.
2. Read the conversation, then write your answer at the bottom and send it. Tick **and close** to file the thread after sending.
3. Use the filters to narrow the list: **Open, Unread, Pending, Closed, All**, **Email / WhatsApp**, and **Assigned to me**.
4. **Assign** a thread to a colleague, or mark it **Pending** (you are waiting on them) or **Closed**.

## Where messages come from

* **Email replies** from people you know arrive automatically (via IMAP). Quoted history is cut off.
* **Campaign emails** you sent appear in the same thread.
* **WhatsApp** messages arrive in real time. A message from a new number creates a person automatically.

## WhatsApp's 24-hour rule

You can send free text only within 24 hours of the person's last message. After that WhatsApp requires an **approved template**, created in your Meta Business account and registered under [Templates](/help/templates) with channel WhatsApp. The composer tells you which applies. Values for the template's placeholders go in "template values", separated by `|`.

## Good to know

* A reply on any channel stops that person's automatic campaign emails.
* Messages are not sent to people marked **Do not contact**, opted out, or on the [Do not contact](/help/suppression) list.
* Failed send attempts stay visible in the thread.
